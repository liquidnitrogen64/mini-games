# Minigames
a collection of mini games, and things to waste your time on when you're bored! I'm going to work on one at a time, so each week there will (hopefully) be either a new minigame or an update to a previous one. Currently, there is a choose-your-own-adventure(CYOA) game, called Tales of the Tea Merchant.
# Tales of the Tea Merchant
Tales of the Tea Merchant is a story about an unnamed Teamaster, who either serves the common folk, solving problems and providing guidance in a small town, or gets tangled in a web of political intrigue and war. (NOTE: THE BETTER EXPANDED STORY IS CURRENTLY THE POLITICAL INTRIGUE.)
It is up to you to choose your path! Survival is not guaranteed!

Here's a handy guide of the kingdoms + major players!
Cult of the Tea: has largely remained unbiased, and has yet to express any direct support for anyone.
The Camellian Empire: Is currently attacking Kilner City, which in response has formed a federation with similarly-sized allies in order to resist the Empire. The Empire is allied with the Assam Kingdom, but has yet to recieve any aid in their war effort.
Assam Kingdom: Is allied with the Camellian Empire, but only maintains this relationship in order to not be conquered immediately. Has no desire to get involved in the Camellian/Kanton War.
The Kanton Federation: A group of allied small countries and city-states. This arrangement was created after the Camellian Empire first attempted to conquer Kilner City.
    Contributions:
        Kilner City: Supplies most of the funds and supplies for the war effort.
        Rooibos Polis: Organized the legal documents of the Kanton Federation, sends delegates and diplomats in attempt to resolve the conflict
        The State of Shennong: Militaristic state that deals with most of the manpower and war strategy
